Do you customize souvenirs?

Yes. Products and packages can be customized according to your requirements, event and branding.

What events do you cater for?

We cater to weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events, celebrations and other special occasions.

Yes. Bulk and corporate enquiries are welcome. Share your quantity and requirements for a tailored quote.

Yes. You can discuss your logo, preferred colours, names, messages and other customization requirements with us.

Yes. Tell us your preferred budget and what you want the package to achieve, and we'll recommend suitable options.
